About

I'm a Salesforce Solution Architect and Customer Success Manager with over ten years of experience implementing, optimising, and scaling Salesforce across complex enterprise environments. Currently at Salesforce itself, I help signature customers extract maximum value from their investments — from onboarding through to strategic renewal.

My background spans both the technical and functional sides of CRM: I've designed loyalty platforms, integrated multi-cloud ecosystems with SAP and Data Lake, led Agentforce and AI implementations, and managed cross-functional teams through full product lifecycles. I approach every engagement as a product challenge — understanding the business deeply before prescribing solutions.

Outside the platform, I hold an MBA in Finance from SIBM Bangalore and a B.Tech in Computer Science, and I've published research on Portfolio Management. I bring the same analytical rigour to Salesforce architecture as to financial modelling.
